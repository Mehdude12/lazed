    It does the homework, too.

    Lazed doesn't just fill a template. It looks things up, checks its own work and keeps going until the file is right.

    Looks things up

    Lazed can search the web, read Wikipedia and open the links you paste, so your documents use real, current facts instead of guesses.

    Finds photos that exist

    Pictures are checked before they go into your file, and photo credits are included where the source asks for them.

    Checks its own work

    Slides, pages and websites are rendered and inspected for clipped text, empty areas and broken layouts, then fixed before you see them.

    Edits what you already have

    Attach up to five files and five images, then ask for changes. Lazed reads Word, PowerPoint, Excel, code and text files.

    Questions people ask.

    Can't find your answer? Email support@lazed.co.in.

    Which computers does Lazed run on?

    Windows PCs and Macs. Lazed is a desktop app, so it doesn't run on phones or tablets. The download page highlights the right file for your computer. On a Mac, choose Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) or Intel; the page shows how to tell which one you have.

    Do I need an internet connection?

    Yes. Your request is sent to our servers, where the AI does the work. The finished file is then saved to the folder you chose on your computer.

    What counts as one file?

    A message that successfully creates or edits a file uses one of your 15 files, however many rounds of building and fixing it takes. Chatting, research and requests that fail don't use a file.

    Does my plan renew by itself?

    No. Each payment gives you 30 days. When they end, or when you've used all 15 files, you choose whether to pay again. We never charge you automatically.

    I paid, but Lazed still says my plan is inactive.

    Access switches on automatically once your payment is confirmed, which usually takes a few minutes. If it hasn't happened after that, email support@lazed.co.in from your account address with your payment confirmation.

    Can Lazed edit a file I already have?

    Yes. Attach up to five files (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, code or text) and up to five images, then say what to change. Word, PowerPoint and Excel files are rebuilt in Lazed's own design, so very custom formatting in the original may change.

    Can I use my account on more than one computer?

    Yes. Your plan belongs to your account, so you can log in on any of your own computers. Please don't share your login with other people.

    Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

    Email support@lazed.co.in. The support page also lists fixes for the most common problems.
