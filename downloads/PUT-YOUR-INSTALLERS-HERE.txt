Put your finished Mac installers in this folder, with exactly these names:

  Lazed-macOS-silicon.dmg    the Mac installer, Apple Silicon (made on an Apple Silicon Mac by desktop-app/build_mac.sh)
  Lazed-macOS-intel.dmg      the Mac installer, Intel         (made on an Intel Mac by desktop-app/build_mac.sh)

build_mac.sh saves its result as Lazed-macOS.dmg; rename it to the matching name above.
Until a file exists, its button on the Download page shows "Coming soon". To use different file names,
change them in js/config.js.

Windows is not here any more: Lazed for Windows is published in the Microsoft Store (see js/config.js,
windows.store.productId). You can delete the old Lazed-Windows.exe from this folder.
