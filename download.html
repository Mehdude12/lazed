      Not sure which Mac you have?
      1. Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen, then About This Mac.
      2. If you see Chip followed by Apple M1, M2, M3 or similar, choose Apple Silicon.
      3. If you see Processor followed by Intel, choose Intel.

      The Apple Silicon version will not open on an Intel Mac. The Intel version can still run on a newer Mac, but macOS may show a warning that it is out of date, so use the Apple Silicon version there.

      Installing Lazed

      It takes about a minute. Only download Lazed from this website.

      On Windows

      1. Open the downloaded installer, Lazed-Windows.exe.

      2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

      3. Follow the installer, then open Lazed from the Start menu (or the desktop shortcut if you chose one) and choose Sign up.

      On Mac

      1. Open the downloaded Lazed .dmg file, then drag Lazed into the Applications folder.

      2. Open Lazed from Applications. Your Mac will say it can’t verify the app. Click Done.

      3. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Lazed. Confirm, and Lazed opens. You only need to do this once. On older versions of macOS you can instead Control-click Lazed and choose Open.

      After you install

      Your first file is a few minutes away.

      1. Sign up. Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters.

      2. Verify your email. Type the 6-digit code we send you. It works for 10 minutes, and you can ask for a new one after a minute.

      3. Activate your plan. Read the plan rules, then pay through PayU. The price is shown at checkout.

      4. Ask for a file. Choose where files are saved, describe what you need, and send.

      Why does my computer show a warning?

      Windows and macOS warn about apps they don't recognise yet, and that includes new apps from smaller publishers. The steps above are the standard way to open an app you trust. If you are unsure that your download is genuine, check the file's checksum when one is shown on this page, or ask us at support@lazed.co.in before you open it.
